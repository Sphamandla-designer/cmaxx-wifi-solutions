Problems we can assess

A symptom is a starting point, not a diagnosis. We can assess supported systems where devices fail to pair, disappear from an app, stop responding to commands or behave differently after a network or account change.

What you notice

One device is offline

What may need checking: power, local signal, pairing, device state, firmware or a manufacturer-side issue.

What you notice

Several devices disappeared

What may need checking: router or WiFi changes, a required hub, account ownership or a shared network dependency.

What you notice

Voice control fails but the app works

What may need checking: the supported integration, permissions, device naming, account connection or platform availability.

What you notice

A routine does not run

What may need checking: its trigger, conditions, participating devices, schedule, permissions or a changed platform feature.

What you notice

Music zones or TVs are unreliable

What may need checking: connection quality, discovery, supported grouping, device configuration and the relevant service.

What you notice

Equipment changed or a new occupant moved in

What may need checking: authorised account transfer, supported setup, network details and removal of old access.

These are possible causes, not remote conclusions about your equipment. The service is for supported-device setup and diagnosis; it does not automatically include electronics repair, electrical fault finding or programming every proprietary automation system.

For network-wide faults, see WiFi Assessment & Troubleshooting. For camera hardware or recording faults, see CCTV & Security Solutions.

Describe My Smart-Home Problem

Find out whether the problem is the device, network or integration

We first clarify what stopped working, when it last worked and what changed. A device working in its own app but not through voice control requires a different investigation from an entire home losing connectivity.

The agreed checks may cover power and device availability, the relevant WiFi or wired connection, hub status, authorised account access, supported app integration and the specific routine or command that fails. We compare the observed problem with the functions the equipment actually supports.

We aim to preserve a working configuration. Factory resets, account unlinking or firmware changes can remove settings or interrupt other functions, so they should not be the automatic first step. Where a disruptive change is needed, the effect and recovery approach should be agreed before it is made.

After diagnosis, we explain the findings and practical next step: a configuration correction, a network improvement, a supported replacement, specialist contractor work or a manufacturer-support case. Some failures cannot be resolved through configuration alone.

Our diagnostic order

  1. Symptom

    What stopped working, when, and what changed.

  2. Connection

    Power, WiFi or wired connection at the device.

  3. Controller / account

    Hub status and authorised account access.

  4. Function

    The routine, command or integration that fails.

  5. Test

    Confirm the agreed functions now work.

Request a Supported-System Assessment

Configure existing devices and make supported upgrades easier

Support is not only for a failure. We can assess supported devices that need initial pairing, clearer names, suitable room groups, household permissions or a practical routine.

A router replacement, WiFi password change, new phone or changed account can affect the way existing devices are managed. A planned change can reduce avoidable disruption by identifying hubs, connection details and ownership before settings are altered.

When adding a new product, we check the intended function, platform and connection requirements instead of assuming it will work because the brand or logo looks familiar. Existing equipment is retained where it remains suitable; replacement is not the default answer.

Please do not

Smart-home support FAQs

My smart device is offline. Does it need replacing?

Not necessarily. Power, the local connection, the required hub, app access or a service outage may be involved. We assess supported equipment and explain whether configuration, networking, manufacturer assistance or replacement is appropriate. An offline label alone does not establish that the device has failed.

Can you reconnect devices after a router or WiFi password change?

We can assess and reconfigure supported devices within an agreed scope. The method depends on the product and whether it allows network details to be changed without a reset. We identify accounts, hubs and affected functions first so that a connection change does not unnecessarily remove other settings.

Why does the device work in its own app but not with Alexa or Google Home?

The device connection may be working while the link to the voice platform is not. Account authorisation, supported functions, device naming or platform availability may need checking. Some features are only available in the manufacturer app, so the desired command also needs to be verified against actual support.

Can you fix an automation that runs at the wrong time?

We can assess a supported routine, including its schedule, time zone, trigger, conditions and participating devices. A missed sensor event or offline controller may be relevant. The solution depends on the cause and platform, and we explain how to adjust or disable the routine where supported.

Should I factory-reset everything before calling?

No. A reset can remove pairing, rooms, scenes or account relationships and may make diagnosis harder. Record the symptoms and recent changes instead. Disruptive changes should be considered only after the effect on the rest of the setup and the recovery process are understood.

Do you support every brand or automation platform?

No universal support promise is made. Share the model numbers, apps and desired functions so we can determine whether the job fits the supported scope. Some systems require an authorised manufacturer specialist, discontinued features cannot simply be restored, and physical repairs may be a separate service.

Can support be provided remotely?

Some supported configuration problems can be investigated remotely with authorised access and a suitable connection. Physical wiring, coverage, power or hardware issues may require an on-site visit. We confirm the access method and scope rather than assuming remote assistance can solve every problem.

Can you help when I move into a house with an existing system?

We can assess supported equipment and an authorised ownership-transfer process. The previous owner or manufacturer may need to release accounts or equipment. We do not bypass access controls. A clear device record and removal of old permissions are important parts of establishing legitimate control.

Is a WiFi fault covered by this service?

We can identify whether the smart-home symptom points to the network. More extensive coverage, router or cabling work belongs in an agreed WiFi troubleshooting or installation scope. The quotation should make that distinction clear rather than implying a configuration visit includes rebuilding the network.

What does a troubleshooting visit cost?

The cost depends on location, the supported equipment, the type of investigation and whether on-site work is needed. Assessment or call-out charges and additional work are confirmed before booking or proceeding. No free visit, fixed duration or guaranteed repair is implied by the enquiry form.
